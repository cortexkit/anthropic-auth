## Changes

- **OpenCode 2 keeps waiting out a quota limit.** When every Claude account is out of quota and Anthropic says when the limit resets, OpenCode 2 now keeps waiting in steps of up to 15 minutes until the reset, then continues the turn. Before, it waited once and then ended the turn while still saying "Retrying every 15 minutes". When the wait cannot reach the reset, or OpenCode itself stops retrying, the turn now says that it has stopped and when to send the message again. ([#817](https://github.com/cortexkit/anthropic-auth/issues/817))
- **Clear stop messages on every host.** When a turn ends because of quota, OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 and Pi now all say "This turn has stopped" and give the time to send the message again, or say that the reset time is unknown.
- **The settings move is no longer held up by old leftover files.** The one-time move to the shared settings file waited for files left behind by older plugin processes. When a process number had since been reused by an unrelated program, the move waited until the next restart of the computer. That case is now recognised.
- **OpenCode cleans up old sidebar connection files.** Files left behind by OpenCode processes that have exited are now removed at startup, at most once a minute. Files of running processes are never touched.
