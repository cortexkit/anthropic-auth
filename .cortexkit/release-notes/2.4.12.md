## Fixes

- **Keep-warm saves only accepted requests.** Keep-warm saved each session's request before Anthropic answered it. A rejected request, such as one that is too long, replaced the session's saved request, and making room for it under the memory limit pushed other warm sessions out of keep-warm, so each of them paid a full cache write on its next turn. Keep-warm now saves a request only once Anthropic accepts it; a rejected, failed or cancelled request leaves the previous one in place. This applies to OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 and Pi. (#591)

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and Pi and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
