## Fixes

- **A Claude request no longer hangs a turn.** The plugin's quota check, account-profile lookup and token refresh had no time limit, and the work before a request is sent ignored OpenCode's own abort. So one unanswered call could hold a turn open indefinitely. Now OpenCode's five-minute wait ends the request on OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 and Pi, each of those account checks gives up after 30 seconds, and the WebSocket relay honours the abort too. A token refresh already under way still finishes in the background, and a request abandoned before Claude answers doesn't change its session's account.
- **OpenCode 2 keep-warm sessions survive plugin updates.** Sessions saved for restart were deleted at the first restart after a plugin update, so idle sessions lost their warm cache. They are now restored, and each saved session dropped at startup is logged with the reason. (#585)
- **State-file move:** records left by processes from before the computer's last restart no longer hold back the upcoming move of the plugin's state files, even when their process number has been reused.

## Docs

- The vault guide explains that a second OpenCode or Pi installation is approved under its own `--name`, and that its grant names that same name.

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and Pi and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
