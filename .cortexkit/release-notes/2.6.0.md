## anthropic-auth 2.6.0

### New

- **Hosts launched by Thalamus use its vault relay.** When Thalamus starts OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 or Pi, the plugin reads your Claude accounts through Thalamus's vault relay, with no separate enrollment. `/claude` shows **Served by Thalamus**. Sessions keep their accounts, routing and quota readings when a host moves under Thalamus. Outside Thalamus nothing changes.
- **Prime works on Pi.** Pi now starts the five-hour window when Prime is on, as OpenCode does. Before, the Prime setting had no effect on Pi.

### Fixes

- **Message threads survive a login refresh.** On OpenCode 2 and Pi, and in local mode on OpenCode 1, a routine login refresh no longer ends the conversation's message thread, so the next request doesn't resend the whole conversation. Debug logs now name the reason whenever a conversation starts a new thread, without logging its contents or credentials.
- **Prime fires once per account.** Prime no longer primes the same Claude account twice when OpenCode and Pi, or two OpenCode installs, use separate account files.
- **Faster shutdown.** OpenCode and Pi no longer pause for up to 15 seconds at shutdown when another process holds the account bookkeeping file.

Still on common-auth 0.40.0.
