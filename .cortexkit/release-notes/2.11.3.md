## Changes

- **A turn survives a short vault restart (Thalamus).** When the vault can't be reached and the plugin has no kept sign-in for the account, a request now waits up to 10 seconds, once per outage, for the vault to come back instead of failing the turn at once. Each connection attempt still ends after 2 seconds. Outside Thalamus nothing changes. (common-auth 0.48.0)
- **The apps can show why a request is waiting (Thalamus).** On OpenCode 2 and Pi, the plugin now reports waits of 2 seconds or more: an account limit resetting, every account at its limit, a retry after a rate limit, or a hold from shared routing. The report carries a fixed message and never account names or provider text. Reports never delay a request.
- **Shared routing keeps a session on its warm account.** On OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 and Pi, each routing request names the account the session is already pinned to, when that account can still serve. Routing can then prefer it and keep the prompt cache.

## Fixes

- **Pi waits at most 60 seconds after an unconfirmed rate limit.** When a 429's headers don't confirm an exhausted limit, every host retries the same account at most twice per turn. OpenCode capped each wait at 60 seconds, but Pi passed Anthropic's own retry time through and could wait up to 15 minutes. Pi now uses the same 60-second cap.
