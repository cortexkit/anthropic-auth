## Changes

- **Keep-warm survives server restarts under Thalamus.** A restored session keeps warming under its saved session policy until the runner can supply it again. Before this, a restart dropped every saved session, and idle heads rewrote their whole prompt about an hour later.
- **One slow keep-warm send no longer ends warming.** Due warms run up to four at a time, one per session, and restored sessions still start at least 3 seconds apart.
- **Planned keep-warm starts 5 minutes before the cache expires**, which leaves room to retry a slow send.
- **Every real turn now replaces or ends its session's keep-warm**, including API-key turns and, on Pi, turns on other providers. No warm is sent while a turn is in flight.
- **Clearer stop reasons.** A warm that can't finish before the cache expires now reports `deadline-missed` rather than `cost-cap`. Sends whose reply was lost count toward the `auto` spending limit.
- Pi releases its keep-warm sessions when it shuts down. They resume from their restart files in the next session.
- Updated the shared authentication library to common-auth 0.51.0.
