## Changes

- **OpenCode 2: retries try every account.** After a rate limit, a retry now tries accounts that haven't refused the request yet before going back to one that did. Accounts confirmed out of quota are skipped. Before, a retry could bounce between two accounts and never try a third. (#862, reported by @iceteaSA)
- **OpenCode 2: fresh weekly model limits and credits in the sidebar.** For the account serving your requests, the model-scoped weekly windows and credits now stay current while response headers keep arriving: from Insula in vault mode, or from the regular usage poll in local mode. The sidebar no longer shows a growing "checked … ago" for that account. No extra usage requests are sent. (#864, reported by @iceteaSA)
- **OpenCode 1 and 2: safety fallback logging.** Every step that Anthropic's safety fallback serves now writes one log line with the requested and serving models and per-attempt token counts, never conversation text. OpenCode 2 previously logged nothing. The fallback notice no longer says follow-up requests stay on the fallback model for about an hour. (#861, reported by @iceteaSA)
- **OpenCode 2 and Pi: prompt cache kept across host restarts.** After a host restart, the first full request of a threaded session now reuses the cache boundary of the previous full request, instead of rewriting most of the conversation. The plugin stores only positions and hashes per session, never message text.
