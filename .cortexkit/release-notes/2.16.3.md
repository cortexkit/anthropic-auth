## Fixes

- **Sticky-balanced routing: Opus no longer gets a false quota-floor refusal.** A new Opus session first narrowed its choice to accounts whose Fable quota was used up, and only then checked how much quota each had left. When every such account sat exactly at its quota floor, the session was refused with "quota floor", even while other accounts had plenty of quota. The plugin now checks every account's remaining quota first, and prefers Fable-exhausted accounts only among those that can still take a new session. Fixes #852.
