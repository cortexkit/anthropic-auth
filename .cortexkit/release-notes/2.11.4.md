## Fixes

- **Billing reports for resent requests (OpenCode 2, Thalamus).** When Anthropic rejects a message-thread continuation, the plugin resends the full request. If OpenCode 2 finished the rejected attempt before the resend returned, the resend's billing report was lost, and its reply had no billing row. Each physical send now keeps its own report: `failed` for the rejected continuation, and `completed` with usage for the resend. Requests sent to Anthropic do not change.
