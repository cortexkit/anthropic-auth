## anthropic-auth 2.7.0

### New

- **Per-session cache policy from Thalamus.** When Thalamus starts a host, it can now choose each Claude login session's prompt-cache lifetime (none, 5 minutes or 1 hour) and its keep-warm mode (off, during set hours, always, or auto). The plugin applies the lifetime to every cache marker it sends. It reports to Thalamus each time a session's cache is refreshed and when keep-warm stops, so Thalamus knows when a cache has really expired. Sessions without a policy keep using your own `/claude` cache settings.
- **Keep-warm "always" has a limit.** With keep-warm set to always, a session that sits idle now stops being warmed after 24 warm attempts without a real request. Before, it was warmed until the process exited. This applies outside Thalamus too, and also to the warms that keep the original model's cache alive during an Opus 4.8 recovery round. Your next real request starts a new round.
- **"Auto" keep-warm stops before it costs more than it saves.** For Thalamus sessions in auto mode, warming stops before the summed cost of the warms would reach one cache rewrite. The warm count and its cost are saved before each warm is sent, so a restart can't reset them.

### Fixes

- **Restored sessions are warmed on time after a restart.** A session restored with little cache left was only checked a minute after start, and due sessions were warmed in the order they were saved, so a near-expiry one could be dropped and rewrite its whole cache on the next turn. Keep-warm now schedules each warm for its due time and warms the closest deadline first ([#669](https://github.com/cortexkit/anthropic-auth/issues/669)).
- **OpenCode 1 HTTP relay sessions stay warm.** On OpenCode 1, sessions using the HTTP relay are now kept warm even when the relayed response carries no request id.

Now on common-auth 0.42.0.
